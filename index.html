Next DevOps

A Windows desktop app for development teams: work items, code, builds, mail and calendar together in one window.

What Next DevOps does

  • Work items, boards and sprints from Azure DevOps.
  • Git repositories, builds and deployments.
  • Your work mail and calendar (Microsoft Exchange).
  • An AI Assistant that helps with all of the above, when you ask it.

Your personal Google Calendar, beside your work calendar

If you choose to connect your own Google account (Settings → Calendar → Google → Connect), Next DevOps shows your Google Calendar events next to your work meetings, and lets you add, change, move and delete them, reply to invitations, and get reminders — all from the same calendar.

Next DevOps asks Google for these permissions only:

  • See the list of your calendars — to show them in the Calendars list, with their names and colours.
  • View and edit events on your calendars — to show your events and to add, change and delete the ones you choose.
  • See your free / busy times — only if you turn on “Keep Google busy times free in Find a time”.

Your calendar data goes only between your PC and Google. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Calendar → Google → Disconnect.
